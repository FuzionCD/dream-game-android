# NOTICE

*I Keep Having This Dream* was originally created by **Alex Kuptsov**, developing as Fireflame Games, and released for iOS. It was delisted around 2018 (unsure on exact date) and has since been unavailable anywhere.

This Android project is a good-faith **preservation** of that otherwise-lost game: a ground-up reimplementation built from the original assets. Those assets (art, sound, and music) come from the iOS release and remain the property of their respective rights holders. No ownership of them is claimed here.

Before publishing, I tried every channel I could find to reach the original author, and all were dead. **If you are Alex, or you hold rights to these non-code assets, please reach out.** I will gladly seek your blessing, transfer the project, or take it down at your request.

Contact: open an issue on this repository.
